Dispute Resolution

Dispute Resolution Policy

This policy outlines the standards governing arbitrators and the requirements for peers entering the dispute resolution process, which is the mechanism built into the Haveno protocol in order to resolve conflicts between peers.

Part 1: Standards Governing Arbitrators on Monocle Network

1.1 Confidentiality

Arbitrators will not disclose peers' personal information or trade details. If a peer requests to review evidence, it will only be shared with the other peer's consent.

1.2 Guidance and Resources

Arbitrators will direct peers to appropriate resources, including trading rules, dispute resolution guidelines, and relevant documentation to help them understand the process.

1.3 Competence and Knowledge

Arbitrators are expected to possess the necessary competence and knowledge to resolve the dispute, or to acquire the necessary knowledge and consult appropriate resources before rendering a decision.

1.4 Fairness, Independence and Impartiality

Arbitrators will act fairly, independently, and impartially throughout the dispute resolution process. No party will be given undue advantage, and decisions will be based solely on the evidence presented and the applicable network rules.

1.5 Written Decisions

Arbitrators will record their decision in writing at the end of the dispute resolution process, and make this available only to the peers involved in the dispute. The written decision will include the reasoning behind the determination.

1.6 Response Time

Arbitrators normally respond within 12 hours. The official deadline is 48 hours, which is rarely needed. If peers have not received a response after 48 hours they should reach out on Monocle Network SimpleX rooms to make arbitrators aware.

1.7 Minimum Case Duration

When one peer is unresponsive, arbitrators must keep dispute cases open for a minimum of 48 hours. This ensures peers are given adequate time to participate in the dispute process and provide evidence.

Part 2: What Arbitrators Expect from Peers

2.1 Respectful Communication

Peers will maintain a friendly and respectful manner. Harassment, abusive language, and hostile behavior are not acceptable.

2.2 Awareness of Network Rules

To mitigate the likelihood of disputes, participants should familiarize themselves with network trading rules before engaging in trades on Monocle Network.

2.3 Awareness of Dispute Resolution Process

Peers will familiarize themselves with Monocle's dispute resolution process, including the steps involved, timelines, and their responsibilities as parties to the dispute.

2.4 Provision of Information

Peers will provide arbitrators with the necessary information when opening a dispute. This includes transaction information, PDFs, screenshots, and any other relevant evidence.

2.5 Response Time

Peers will respond to messages from arbitrators within 24 hours. Delays in responding will result in the dispute resolution proceeding without the non-responsive party's input.

Part 3: Appeals Policy

Appeals allow peers to have an arbitrator's decision reviewed by a different arbitrator, ensuring fairness and consistency in the dispute resolution process.

For more information, see the Appeals Policy.
