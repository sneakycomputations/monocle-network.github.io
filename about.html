About

Vision

Monocle's vision is to provide people with a safe, private and transparent way to buy and sell Monero.

Monocle is a Bootstrapped Network

Monocle Network is a community-driven open-source network. It was founded with the belief that:

  • People should have a safe and private way to buy and sell Monero.
  • Contributors who dedicate their time and skills to this vision deserve to be compensated.
  • The wider community deserves to have transparency into the operations of Monocle Network.

For more information see the bootstrapped operations policy.

Aims

  • Put peers at the forefront of decision making.
  • Maintain low, transparent, and capped network fees.
  • Provide responsive, multi-channel support that respects peers' time.
  • Provide transparency regarding network fees and reserve wallets through cryptographic proof, including view keys, transaction IDs, transaction keys, and a published ledger of all relevant transactions.
  • Build a positive, active community where peers feel heard and valued.
  • Create compensated contribution opportunities in Monero for community members, node operators, support team members, and more.
  • Be net contributors to the Haveno community, the wider Monero ecosystem, and the open-source ecosystem.
  • Expand Haveno's reach to new peers while providing existing peers with more choice.
  • Support open-source projects that lack a direct path to revenue but are vital to the ecosystem.
  • Commit to peers through actions, not words.

 

Monocle's Value Proposition

Same, same but different

All Haveno networks share the same codebase, protocol, and core features. So why choose Monocle?

Because what sets Monocle Network apart is how it operates.

Like every Haveno network, Monocle provides private Monero trading over Tor, and no KYC, no accounts, no identity requirements.

 

What Monocle Brings:

Radical Transparency

Monocle publishes view keys for all network fee and reserve wallets. You can verify all transactions and balances. 33% of all network fees are distributed to the three publicly auditable reserves.

Peer Security Reserve

Monocle maintains a Peer Security Reserve to protect peers in the event of an exploit. The reserve wallet is publicly auditable via view key.

Haveno Network Development Grant

Monocle Network supports the continued development and improvement of the Haveno project.

Open Source Sustainability Grant

Monocle Network provides grants to projects that keep the ecosystem sustainable.

Active Community

Clear pathways for contributors to get involved and get compensated in Monero. Changes are discussed publicly before implementation. Your feedback shapes the network.

Responsive Support

Multi-channel support with quick response times via messages in the group.

Low, Capped Network Fees

Fees capped at 0.3% per trade (0.1% Maker and 0.2% Taker) for the first 12 months. After that period, any proposed changes will be publicly discussed. At least 30 days’ notice will be given for any changes. The combined Maker and Taker fees are not expected to ever exceed 1% of the trade amount. Monocle believes lower network fees mean more trades, which means a healthier network.
