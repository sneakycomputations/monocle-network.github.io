Privacy Policy

Monocle Network is committed to protecting the privacy of its peers. This policy describes what information Monocle collects, how it is used, and the choices peers have regarding their data.

Monocle operates on a simple principle: peer privacy is non-negotiable, while network operations must be transparent.

1. Overview

Monocle Network facilitates peer-to-peer Monero trading. The network is designed so that peers can buy and sell Monero privately, without providing personal information, creating accounts, or submitting to identity verification.

Monocle does not collect, store, or process personal data as a condition of using the network.

2. Information Monocle Does Not Collect

Monocle does not require or collect:

  • Personal identification - No KYC, no identity documents, no real names
  • Account information - No accounts, no email addresses, no phone numbers
  • Financial information - No bank details, no fiat payment routing through Monocle
  • Transaction records of peers - Monero transactions are private; Monocle cannot see peer balances or transaction histories
  • IP addresses - All connections route through Tor; Monocle does not log originating IP addresses
  • Location data - Monocle does not track or store geographic information

3. Information Visible to Monocle

As a peer-to-peer network, some information is inherently visible to the network during operation:

3.1 On-Network Activity

  • Trade offers - Peers who post offers make certain information visible to other peers on the network, including payment methods and pricing
  • Trade protocols - When peers trade, the Haveno protocol facilitates communication between the two parties. Monocle does not mediate this communication outside of the dispute process.
  • Dispute information - If a dispute arises, information shared during dispute resolution is visible to the assigned arbitrator. See Section 5.

3.2 Network Fee and Reserve Wallets

Monocle operates with radical transparency regarding network finances:

  • View keys for the network fee address and all community reserve wallets are publicly published
  • Transaction IDs and secret transaction keys for distributions from these wallets are published
  • Anyone can verify network fee collection totals and reserve wallet balances on-chain

This transparency applies only to Monocle's operational wallets. Peer wallets are private.

3.3 Contributor Compensation

  • Contributors who receive compensation from Monocle publish a receive address and view key
  • Transaction IDs and transaction view keys for compensation disbursements are published on GitHub for community verification
  • Contributor GitHub usernames and associated platform identifiers are public

Contributors choose to participate in this transparency as a condition of compensation.

4. Community Spaces

Monocle operates community spaces on third-party platforms:

  • SimpleX
  • Matrix
  • Community forum
  • Telegram
  • Reddit
  • GitHub

Each platform has its own privacy policy and data handling practices. Monocle does not control the data policies of these third-party services. Peers should review the privacy policies of any platform they use to interact with Monocle's community.

Monocle's community spaces are moderated. Moderators may remove messages that violate community guidelines, but Monocle does not archive private messages between peers.

5. Dispute Resolution

In the event of a trade dispute, peers may engage Monocle's dispute resolution process:

  • Information provided during dispute resolution (transaction details, evidence, screenshots) is visible to the assigned arbitrator
  • Arbitrators are bound by confidentiality obligations and will not disclose personal information or dispute details
  • A peer may request to review evidence provided by the other peer, but only with that peer's consent
  • Dispute resolution decisions are recorded in writing and shared only with the peers involved in the dispute
  • Monocle does not publish dispute resolution outcomes or identifying details publicly

6. Data Retention

Monocle retains the minimum data necessary for network operations:

Data Type Retention Period
Network fee wallet transactions Permanent (publicly visible via view keys)
Reserve wallet transactions Permanent (publicly visible via view keys)
Contributor compensation records Permanent (publicly visible on GitHub)
Dispute resolution records Retained only for the duration of the dispute and appeal process
Community space messages Controlled by each third-party platform

Monocle does not maintain databases of peer activity, trade histories, or personal information.

7. Data Monocle Publishes

Transparency is a core value. The following information is publicly available:

  • Network fee wallet address and view key
  • Community reserve wallet addresses and view keys
  • Transaction IDs and secret transaction keys for all distributions from network fee and reserve wallets
  • Contributor compensation requests and disbursements on GitHub
  • Network fee collection totals

All of this information relates to network operations, not peer activity.

8. Third-Party Software

Monocle Network runs on Haveno's free and open-source software. The Haveno application:

  • Generates a Monero wallet locally on the peer's device
  • Routes all connections through Tor
  • Does not transmit personal data to Monocle or any central server
  • Stores all wallet data locally on the peer's device

Peers should review the Haveno project's documentation for details on how the software handles data.

9. Peer Responsibilities

Peers are responsible for:

  • Securing their own wallet - Monocle does not have access to peer wallets, seed phrases, or private keys. Peers must safeguard their own funds
  • Understanding trade risks - Trading on a peer-to-peer network involves risk. Monocle provides dispute resolution but cannot guarantee outcomes
  • Information shared in trades - When trading, peers may need to share payment details with their counterparty directly. This information is outside Monocle's control
  • Community conduct - Peers are responsible for their own behaviour in community spaces and must adhere to each platform's terms of service

10. Changes to This Policy

Monocle may update this privacy policy periodically. Changes will be announced through public community channels and documented in version history. Substantive changes will be published with at least 30 days' notice.
