Community Guidelines

Monocle Network is committed to providing a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free community for everyone. These guidelines outline the expected behaviour for all who participate in Monocle's community spaces.

These guidelines apply to all Monocle-operated community spaces, including SimpleX, Matrix, the community forum, Telegram, Reddit, GitHub, and any other spaces where Monocle community members interact.

1. Purpose

A healthy community is built on mutual respect. Monocle values privacy, transparency, community, security, and censorship resistance. These guidelines exist to ensure that Monocle's community spaces remain places where:

  • Peers feel safe to ask questions, share ideas, and seek support
  • Contributors can collaborate openly and constructively
  • Diverse perspectives are welcomed and valued
  • Disagreements are handled respectfully and productively

2. Expected Behaviour

The following behaviours are expected and encouraged of all community members:

2.1 Be Respectful

  • Treat all community members with respect and consideration
  • Communicate constructively and avoid personal attacks
  • Respect the privacy and pseudonymity of others - do not attempt to deanonymize or identify any peer or contributor
  • Use welcoming language

2.2 Be Honest and Transparent

  • Do not misrepresent yourself, your experience, or your intentions
  • Do not spread misinformation about Monocle, Haveno, Monero, or other community members
  • If you have a conflict of interest, disclose it

2.3 Be Supportive

  • Help new peers who are learning to use the network
  • Share knowledge and experience generously
  • Be patient with those who are new to Monero or peer-to-peer trading
  • Direct peers to appropriate resources when you cannot help directly

2.4 Respect the Space

  • Stay on topic in designated channels and rooms
  • Do not spam, flood, or repeatedly post the same content
  • Do not post content that is illegal in the jurisdictions where Monocle operates
  • Do not use Monocle's community spaces to solicit or advertise services unrelated to Monocle

3. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are unacceptable:

3.1 Harassment and Abuse

  • Personal insults, threats, or intimidation
  • Derogatory comments about race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, disability, religion, or any other personal characteristic
  • Stalking, following, or persistent unwanted contact

3.2 Privacy Violations

  • Attempting to deanonymize, identify, or dox any peer or contributor
  • Sharing or threatening to share personal information about any community member without their consent
  • Recording or screenshotting private conversations without consent, particularly in spaces designed for confidential support
  • Using information shared in confidence to harm or manipulate others

3.3 Fraud and Deception

  • Impersonating Monocle contributors or arbitrators
  • Posting phishing links, malware, or other harmful content
  • Attempting to scam, defraud, or deceive community members
  • Coordinating or facilitating trade manipulation outside of the Haveno protocol

3.4 Disruption

  • Deliberately disrupting community conversations or support channels
  • Trollish behaviour - arguing in bad faith, sealioning, or concern trolling
  • Repeatedly violating community guidelines after being asked to stop
  • Organizing or encouraging others to violate these guidelines

3.5 Illegal Content

  • Posting content that is illegal under applicable law
  • Sharing material that exploits or harms minors
  • Promoting or facilitating illegal activities unrelated to the lawful use of cryptocurrency

4. Scope

These guidelines apply to:

  • All Monocle-operated community spaces (SimpleX, Matrix, forum, Telegram, Reddit, GitHub)
  • Interactions between community members in the context of Monocle Network
  • The conduct of contributors in their Monocle capacity

These guidelines do not apply to:

  • Private communications between peers outside of Monocle's community spaces
  • Conduct on platforms not operated or moderated by Monocle
  • Individual peers acting in personal capacity unrelated to Monocle

5. Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates these guidelines:

5.1 How to Report

  1. In-channel - If you feel safe doing so, politely ask the person to stop. They may be unaware of the impact of their behaviour
  2. Direct message to a moderator - Contact a support contributor or community steward via SimpleX or Matrix
  3. GitHub - Open an issue on the relevant Monocle repository if the violation occurs on GitHub
  4. SimpleX or Matrix - Notify Monocle contributors

Reports should include:

  • A description of the incident
  • When and where it occurred
  • Any relevant screenshots or evidence
  • The nym or identifier of the person involved, if known

5.2 Confidentiality

All reports will be treated confidentially. The identity of the reporter will not be disclosed without their consent.

5.3 Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports a violation in good faith is itself a violation of these guidelines and will be treated accordingly.

6. Consequences

Violations of these guidelines will be addressed proportionally. Possible responses include:

Response When Applied
Informal reminder First minor violation, likely unintentional
Formal warning Repeated violations or more serious first offence
Permanent ban Severe violations such as fraud, doxxing, or threats

6.1 Process

  1. Report is received and reviewed by a support contributor
  2. The accused party is given an opportunity to respond
  3. A decision is made based on the evidence and the severity of the violation
  4. The outcome is communicated to the reporter and the accused
  5. Decisions may be appealed by contacting a different support contributor

6.2 Consistency

Consequences are applied consistently regardless of the identity, status, or tenure of the person involved. No one is exempt from these guidelines.
