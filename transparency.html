Transparency Policy

Monocle recognizes the importance of transparency in building trust, fostering accountability, and promoting community engagement. This Transparency Policy establishes Monocle's commitment to openness, honesty, and integrity across all network operations, including:

  • Finances
  • Code
  • Communications
  • Community Reserves
  • Grants and bounties
  • Network fees

1. Introduction

Monocle Network operates on a simple principle: peer privacy is non-negotiable, while network operations are transparent.

Peers trade privately, while Monocle's own operations remain publicly verifiable. Monocle does not ask peers to trust it; it gives them the tools to verify.

Transparency applies to network operations only. Peer wallets, trades, and activity are private by design and are never published.

2. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

  • Define the principles and practices of transparency that guide Monocle's decision-making, communication, and operational processes
  • Promote a culture of transparency, accountability, and ethical conduct among contributors, peers, and the wider community
  • Enhance trust and confidence in Monocle's operations and published practices

3. Policy Statements

The main rules for the Transparency Policy are outlined here:

  • Open Communication: Maintain open channels of communication with the community, providing timely, accurate, and relevant information on activities, performance, and decisions
  • Honesty and Integrity: Conduct all network operations with honesty, integrity, and ethical behavior, avoiding misrepresentation, deception, or concealment of information
  • Accountability and Responsibility: Hold contributors accountable for their actions, decisions, and outcomes, promoting a culture of responsibility and ownership
  • Accessibility: Ensure that the application, website, and wiki are as accessible as possible
  • Fairness and Equity: Treat all peers and community members fairly, equitably, and impartially

4. Transparency Practices

The following will guide Monocle's practices to ensure complete transparency in all aspects of operations:

  • Disclosure and Reporting: Provide regular and comprehensive disclosures of financial, operational, and decision-making information through published reports, monthly fee collection totals, and on-chain records as applicable
  • Community Engagement: Proactively engage with the community through various channels, such as GitHub, discussion forums, SimpleX and Matrix spaces, to listen to input, address concerns, and build trust
  • Transparency in Decision Making: Ensure transparency in decision-making processes, including rationale, criteria, and outcomes, to enhance understanding and promote trust among the community
  • Data Privacy and Protection: Protect the privacy of network peers
