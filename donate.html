Donate

Monocle Network is building a community-driven peer-to-peer network for Monero, private, transparent, and for the benefit of the community. There are no investors, no venture funding, and no network token: the network is bootstrapped, funded entirely by its own fees, and contributors are compensated only when collected fees allow (see the Bootstrapped Operations Policy).

That model keeps Monocle independent, but it also means growth depends on the community around it. Donations accelerate everything: contributor compensation, security reserves, and Haveno development.

Why donate

  • More choices for Haveno networks - community members who want to see the Haveno ecosystem grow and diversify
  • Shared vision - community members aligned with Monocle's vision, mission, and values: peer privacy, transparent operations, and open-source sustainability
  • Give something back - traders who have benefited from the service and want to support the network that served them
  • Keep contributors working - donations to the contributor donation addresses let contributors be compensated for their work while the network is still growing toward profitability

Preferred: support the contributors

The network is bootstrapped, so contributors are compensated only when sufficient fees have been collected. Donations to the contributor donation addresses go 100% to contributors, funding the work that keeps the network running while it grows.

Every contribution, large or small, directly supports the people behind Monocle.

How you can verify your donation

Monocle's transparency applies to donations as it does to everything else:

  • All donation addresses have published view keys, available on the wiki, the website, and within the application. You can confirm your donation arrived using a view-only wallet.
  • Any spend from the donation addresses is published with transaction IDs and keys, so you can see exactly where donations go (see Transparency).

Donations are voluntary and non-refundable. Donations do not change network fees or the reserve allocation: the 33% Community Reserves split of collected network fees is defined by the Reserve Allocation Policy and applies to all funds received by the network fee address, donations included.

Every donation keeps Monocle independent, transparent, and building. Thank you for supporting the network.
