Resources

Get Involved

Monocle’s vision is to provide people with a safe and private way to buy and sell Monero. If you would like to get involved, reach out by contacting Monocle.

Ways to Get Involved

If you want to go fast, go alone. If you want to go far, go together.

Monocle puts peers at the center of what it does. Without peers, there is no Monocle Network. Listening to their needs and meeting their expectations is central to Monocle's mission.

Monocle welcomes new community members, contributors, and anyone interested in helping build a sustainable Haveno ecosystem for peers, operators, contributors, and the wider Monero community alike.

Ways to get involved with Monocle include:

  • Helping peers with their questions on Monocle's SimpleX and Matrix rooms
  • Posting questions about the network or joining in any discussions on Monocle Network community forum
  • Creating issues on Monocle Network GitHub for any problems you might be experiencing
  • Posting ideas you have for any Haveno Network Development Grant bounties in the community forum
  • Helping out with some of the required operations

Eligibility

Suitable contributors will share Monocle's values:

  • Privacy
  • Transparency
  • Community
  • Security
  • Censorship resistance

Contributors are free to work from wherever they are, without identifying themselves.

Defined Roles

As a distributed peer-to-peer network, geographic diversity across node operators, support contributors, and arbitrators is important to Monocle Network. This ensures 24-hour coverage and operational redundancy.

Support Contributors

Front-facing contributors will be responsible for:

  • Answering support questions on SimpleX, Matrix, the forum, and GitHub.
  • Moderating these venues, removing disruptive behavior, and keeping discussions on-topic.
  • Expanding and maintaining the Monocle Network Wiki.
  • Managing the Monocle subreddit.
  • Promoting Monocle and attracting new peers.
  • Maintaining Monocle's X, Nostr, Reddit, and Telegram accounts.
  • Coordinating bounty operations, including gathering community feedback, posting and tracking bounties, ensuring total commitments remain within available funds, reviewing bounties for closure, and notifying Monocle when disbursements are due.

Node Operators

The more decentralized the network, the better. Monocle needs contributors to operate and maintain its peer-to-peer infrastructure, including:

  • Monero nodes
  • Price nodes
  • Seed nodes

Node operators must be able to provide stable infrastructure, have experience operating services over Tor, and maintain adequate DDoS protection.

Node operators are trusted roles. A proven track record of reliable operation is required.

Markets Operator

Monocle intends to provide a clearnet interface similar to haveno.markets, allowing peers to view the offer book without running the Monocle client.

Contributors interested in developing and maintaining this service are welcome to reach out.

Arbitrators

Given the importance of this role, arbitrators should be chosen from among trusted community members who have demonstrated integrity through prior contributions to the network.

Compensation

All roles are compensated in Monero.

Monocle believes that while peers should have privacy, they should also expect transparency regarding how Monocle Network operates.

Contributor compensation is managed through a transparent, community-visible process. Compensation is disbursed from the Monocle Network fee address, with all transactions publicly recorded on GitHub for verification.

Contributors submit completed work as compensation requests through the compensation repository, including:

  • GitHub username (matching identifiers across SimpleX, Matrix, and other platforms)
  • Role completed (e.g., “Support” or “Monero node operator”)
  • Agreed-upon USD amount for the work
  • Monero address for receiving compensation

Once verified, the transaction ID and key are added to the issue for community confirmation. This provides transparency into both network fees and their allocation to contributors.

Compensation Amount

Compensation will be denominated in Monero and calculated using the XMR/USD exchange rate at the at the time of distribution.

Support Contributors:

$400 USD/month, minimum 10 hours per week

Node Operators:

$50 USD/month per Monero node $50 USD/month per price node $50 USD/month per seed node

Markets Operator:

$50 USD/month

Arbitrators:

$20 USD per arbitration case, 24-hour response expectation

Compensation Schedule

Roles are compensated monthly.

Contributors must submit an issue on Monocle Network's GitHub by the 10th of each month for roles completed during the previous month. Any questions regarding the submitted work should be added as comments to the issue by the 20th.

Compensation is made by the end of the month.

This cycle repeats monthly: work completed during one month is compensated by the end of the following month.

As Monocle is bootstrapping the network, contributions made during the initial phase of the project will be deferred until Monocle generates sufficient revenue to compensate contributors for the work delivered.

Express your Interest

The best way to let Monocle know you want to contribute is through a message on SimpleX. A pinned post lists currently available roles and occasional one-time compensated tasks. If nothing is available, or catches your interest, check back periodically.

If you are interested in a role or task, tell Monocle a little about yourself and why you would be a good fit.

Proof of Work Requirement

Contributor candidates are expected to demonstrate a track record of useful contributions. For example, Monocle may select a community member as a support contributor based on their history of helping peers in Monocle's community channels.

This track record does not need to come from the Monocle community. Relevant examples include:

  • Contributions to another open-source community.
  • Helping peers on the Monero subreddit.
  • Supporting peers in the Haveno Matrix room.

Monocle aims to build a community that puts peers first. If you are already helping the Monero community, consider contributing to Monocle.

First Month as a Contributor

Aspiring contributors are expected to perform their role for one month before becoming eligible for compensation. During this period, they will perform their duties (e.g., provide support, run a node) with assistance from Monocle.

This period allows contributors to familiarize themselves with the role and gives Monocle an opportunity to evaluate their performance before establishing an ongoing collaboration.

Nyms Welcome

Can a privacy-focused peer-to-peer network be transparent? Monocle thinks so, and intends to prove it.

Monocle expects contributors to operate under nyms. Privacy is a core value, and no personal information is required. Contributors only need to provide a nym and an XMR address when requesting compensation.

Can a network operated by nyms earn the trust of the Monero community? Monocle believes so. Earning that trust requires contributors who share Monocle's vision and values.

If your nym already has a track record of useful contributions in the Monero space and you are interested in contributing, let Monocle know.

A new nym is also welcome. In that case, Monocle asks that you first establish a track record of useful contributions under that nym before reaching out.
