Dispute Resolution

On Monocle Network disputes are managed through a transparent process.

Trading Rules

Learning and consistently applying the trading rules helps minimize the risk of conflicts.

Table of Penalties

Defining a penalty for violating existing rules will incentivize the violating peer to follow the rules in the future and compensate the affected peer for the inconvenience.

Dispute Resolution Policy

If conflicts arise, negotiation can and should be conducted privately between peers, but an arbitrator can be asked to assist when no agreement can be reached.

Appeals Policy

A peer who has reason to believe that an arbitrator's decision was incorrect can file an appeal to have the decision reviewed and, where applicable, corrected.
