Canary

This canary is a regularly published statement affirming that Monocle Network has not been subject to any legal process, secret orders, or coercion that would compromise the privacy of its peers.

If this canary is not updated by the expected date, it should be assumed that Monocle Network has received a legal demand that it is prohibited from disclosing.

As of the date published below, Monocle Network confirms that it has **never**:

- - Received a warrant, subpoena, court order, or any other legal process requesting peer data, trade records, or identifying information
- - Received a National Security Letter (NSL) or any equivalent classified legal demand
- - Received a gag order, sealing order, or any legal instruction preventing disclosure of information to peers
- - Been compelled to implement any form of surveillance, tracking, logging, or monitoring of peers
- - Been asked or compelled to implement KYC, identity verification, or account registration
- - Been asked or compelled to hand over any data that could identify peers or their trading activity
- - Been compelled to weaken, bypass, or undermine Monocle's privacy features or security practices
- - Received any legal demand that would require Monocle to act contrary to the principles stated in this canary

**This list is exhaustive.** If any of the above occurs, this canary will not be updated.

What constitutes a dead canary

This canary should be considered dead if any of the following occur:

  • The canary is not updated within 14 days of the expected update date
  • The canary page is removed from the website without explanation
  • The status field changes to anything other than ✅ Active
  • The statement text is materially altered to weaken or narrow the commitments above

A dead canary means peers should assume that one or more of the statements above are no longer true, even if no explicit disclosure is made.

Monocle's Data Position

Monocle Network is designed so that it does not collect the kind of data that legal process typically seeks:

  • No accounts, no KYC, no personal information
  • No IP logging - all connections route through Tor
  • No peer transaction records - Monero transactions are private by default
  • No peer wallet access - Monocle cannot see peer balances or transaction histories

Even if Monocle were to receive a legal demand, the design of the network means there is very little data that could be provided. Monocle's transparency applies to network operations (fee wallets and reserve wallets), not peer activity.

Scope

This canary applies to Monocle Network. It does not cover:

  • The Haveno project or Haveno contributors
  • Third-party platforms used for community spaces (SimpleX, Matrix, Telegram, GitHub)
  • Individual Monocle contributors acting in personal capacity
