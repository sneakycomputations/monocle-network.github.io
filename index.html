Privacy Meets
Transparency

Peers trade privately, while Monocle operates transparently. Your data stays on your device, and Monocle publishes view keys for its own network addresses.

Values

Privacy by design

You control your own information

Monero is at the core of Monocle, so transaction privacy is a native feature. Peers trade directly with one another while maintaining custody of their funds.

Transparency

Peers deserve visibility into how Monocle operates

Monocle publishes view keys for network fee and reserve wallets and discloses how network fees are distributed across development, operations, and reserves.

Community

Together we go further

Monocle listens to peers through bounties, feature requests, and transparent decision-making. New community members and contributors are welcome.

Security

Protection beyond protocol

Alongside the Haveno base layer, Monocle provides a publicly auditable Peer Security Reserve, a vulnerability bounty program, and rapid-response procedures.

Censorship resistance

Multiple networks create resilience

Independent networks serving the same protocol give peers alternatives. Monocle will seek to decentralize the Haveno ecosystem wherever technically feasible.
