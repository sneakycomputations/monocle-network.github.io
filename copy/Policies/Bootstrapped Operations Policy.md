# Bootstrapped Operations Policy

Monocle Network (Monocle) is a bootstrapped network. This means:

- Operations are funded from collected [network fees]()
- Contributor compensation is drawn from network fees, not network reserves
- During early growth, compensation may be deferred until sufficient network fees exist
- All outstanding approved compensation requests are publicly visible in the [compensation repository]()

Monocle Network is committed to [transparency](). This is because the community deserves clarity about network operations. The commitment to radical transparency applies to network fee allocations, not just user privacy.

Contributors should note that compensation may not be immediate. Contributors are encouraged to review current fee collection and outstanding requests before submitting.

The goal of this policy is to explain the bootstrapped nature of the network.

---

## Why is Monocle a Bootstrapped Network?

Monocle Network is a community-driven open-source network. It was founded with the belief that:

* People should have a safe and private way to buy and sell Monero.
* Contributors who dedicate their time and skills to this vision deserve to be compensated.
* The wider community deserves to have transparency into the operations of Monocle Network.

The benefits of bootstrapping the Monocle Network open-source project include:

### Creative Freedom and Control
Having no investors enables Monocle to have a long-term vision and build a Haveno network that is based on community needs rather than quarterly returns. 

### Resource Discipline and Efficiency
Having limited resources means that the network is forced to be lean in its operations. This encourages contributors to use open-source tools, work remotely asynchronously, and validate and match its roadmap with what the community desires. 

### Community Engagement and Trust
All contributors collaborate openly. Aligning growth to the positive impact it has on the community. Monocle users can contribute code, update the wiki, report bugs, and suggest new features. Effectively Monocle's users can act as an extension to its distributed network.

### Faster Time to Market
Leveraging the work done on the existing Haveno network means reduced development costs and the ability to concentrate on operating an effective network for the community.

### Sustainable Network Model
All contributors collaborate openly, fostering loyalty, drive, community engagement, and facilitating organic growth. Success for Monocle Network comes from building a loyal user base that values the services it provides.

### Peer-Funded Growth
Network fees come from peers. By operating a network they want, Monocle can grow organically without requiring external investment. 

### Gradual Scaling
Growth will match network fees. This aligns with peers as the focus is on adding and retaining users by providing the best possible service.

## How Network Fees are Distributed

Network fees collected by Monocle Network are distributed as follows:

* **33% to Community Reserves** - Allocated first before any other distributions.
* **Contributor Compensation** - Distributed from the remaining network fee balance until all approved requests are satisfied.
* **Network Maintainers** - Remainder after contributor compensation.

### Why 33% of Network Fees are Distributed to Community Reserves

Directing 33% of network fees to community reserves enables:

**Transparent priorities** - Peer protection, development, and open-source support are prioritized.
**Sustainability protection** - Security reserves and development grants remain intact regardless of temporary cost fluctuation.
**Ecosystem commitment** - Reserves receive allocation regardless of commissioned work volume.



## Community Reserves

The 33% of network fees allocated first before contributor compensation goes to three community reserves:

| Fund | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Peer Security Reserve | Peer reimbursement in case of exploits |
| Haveno Network Development Grants | Bug bounties and developer grants |
| Open Source Sustainability Grants | Grants to projects vital to the ecosystem |

Internal split allocation may be adjusted to respond to emerging needs.

## Network Maintainers

Network maintainers coordinate network operations and the wider Monocle Network Open Source Project. After contributor compensation, remaining allocations support ongoing management activities.

## How Compensation Works

Monocle Network operates on a bootstrapped model. Operations are funded from collected network fees, not upfront capital. Contributor and maintainer allocations occur after 33% of network fees have been allocated to community reserves.

- If network fee collections are **sufficient**, all approved requests for that cycle are fully funded.
- If **insufficient**, approved requests accumulate and are **funded together once collections are adequate**.
- Each contributor receives allocations at their designated address. Contributors provide wallet view keys for independent verification of receipt.

## Transparency Commitment

All contributor allocations are published openly on the Monocle Network GitHub [Compensation](https://) repository for public audit. While participants deserve strict transaction privacy, they also deserve transparency regarding network operations.

What information is published:

* Role reports
* Compensation requests
* Distribution transaction IDs and keys for contributors
* View keys for fund wallets
