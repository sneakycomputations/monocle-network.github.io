## Privacy Meets Transparency

Peers trade privately, while Monocle operates transparently. Your data never leaves your device, but Monocle Network shares view keys for its own addresses.

## Values

### Privacy by Design
*You control your own information*

Monero is at the core of Monocle, so transaction privacy is a native feature, and peers trade directly with one another while maintaining custody of their own funds.

### Transparency 
*Peers deserve visibility into how Monocle operates*

While Monero provides peers with privacy at the transaction layer, Monocle Network's operations should remain visible. View keys are therefore published for network fee and reserve wallets. Monocle discloses how network fees are distributed across development, operations, and reserves.
 
### Community
*Together we go further*

Monocle Network puts peers at the center of decision-making. Monocle listens to their needs and acts through bounties, feature requests, and transparent decision-making. New community members and contributors are welcome to help build a sustainable Haveno ecosystem for peers and the wider Monero community.
 
### Security
*Protection beyond protocol*

The security of Monocle Network matches that of other Haveno networks at the base layer, with additional proactive protection through the publicly auditable Peer Security Reserve, a bounty program that incentivizes vulnerability discovery, and rapid-response protocols for timely action when issues surface.

### Censorship Resistance
*Multiple networks create resilience*

All Haveno networks have centralized elements. Nonetheless, multiple independent networks serving the same protocol create resilience through competition. If one network neglects its peers or shuts down, peers have alternatives. As Monocle grows, it will seek to decentralize the Haveno ecosystem wherever technically feasible.

More networks mean greater censorship resistance and more options for everyone.


## What Makes Monocle Network Different

### Transparency
Network fees and reserves are made transparent through published view keys. Monocle does not ask for peers' trust, but wants them to verify.

### Low Network Fees
Fees are capped at 0.3% for the first year, and not expected to ever exceed 1%.

### Peer Protection
Monocle allocates 33% of network fees to community reserves, which can be used to reimburse affected peers in the event of an exploit.

### Community-Driven
Suggest changes, build community support, and turn popular ideas into bounties that improve the wider Haveno ecosystem. You can also earn Monero by helping others.

### Active Support
Support contributors aim to reply to requests within a few hours on SimpleX and regularly monitor Matrix, Telegram, and GitHub. The Wiki provides common guides and troubleshooting resources.

## Community Reserves

33% of network fees go to community reserves.

### Peer Security Reserve

In the event of software or protocol vulnerabilities leading to loss of funds, this reserve will help reimburse affected peers.

### Haveno Network Development Grant

This grant will support continued Haveno development through bounties and audits.

### Open Source Sustainability Grant

This grant will advance open-source projects that benefit Monocle and the Monero community.
