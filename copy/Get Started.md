# Get Started

1. [Download](https://) Monocle
2. Install Monocle
3. Start Monocle
4. Back up your seed phrase
5. Send some XMR to your Monocle wallet (at least 0.1 XMR is recommended to get yourself started)
6. Add a payment account, be it fiat, bitcoin or other cryptocurrencies
7. Make or take an offer
8. Complete the trade
9. Join the [community](supportpage)
